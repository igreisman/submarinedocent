SubmarineDocent — FAQ Review

Review, edit, and accept generated FAQ entries into the main corpus

How to use the FAQ Review Tool

Purpose

This tool lets you read, edit, and promote AI-generated FAQ entries. Entries start with a prefix that tells you their origin and review status. Once you are satisfied with an entry you can accept it, which permanently moves it into the main faq_NNN corpus where it will be used by the docent AI.

Entry prefixes

der

der_NNN — Derived from dieselsubs.com source material. AI rewrote existing FAQ content into a new question-and-answer format. Generally reliable but not individually expert-reviewed.

pam

pam_NNN — Generated by the pampanito_docent pipeline from general knowledge. Fills gaps not covered by dieselsubs.com. Treat with caution — some entries contain factual errors and have not been reviewed.

fix

fix_NNN — Originally pam_ entries that have been expert-reviewed and corrected. These are the most reliable generated entries.

Reviewing an entry

1

Use the filter buttons (All / der_ / pam_ / fix_) in the toolbar to narrow the list by prefix, and the search box to find entries by keyword in the title or answer text.

2

Click an entry in the left panel to open it in the editor on the right.

3

Read the Title / Question and Answer Text carefully. Pay attention to specific facts: dates, numbers, equipment names, procedures, and any claims that can be verified against primary sources.

Editing an entry

1

Click into either text field and make your corrections directly. Both fields are free-form text.

2

Click Save changes to write your edits to the corpus file immediately. The entry keeps its current prefix (der_, pam_, or fix_) — saving does not promote it.

3

Click Discard edits at any time before saving to revert everything back to the last saved version.

If you navigate away with unsaved changes, the browser will warn you before leaving the page.

Accepting an entry

When an entry is factually correct, complete, and ready for use by the docent AI, click Accept as faq_….

  • If you have unsaved edits, you will be asked to confirm they should be saved first.
  • A confirmation dialog will appear before the promotion happens.
  • The entry is assigned the next available faq_NNN number and written to the corpus.
  • The entry card turns gray in the list to show it has been accepted. It cannot be edited from this tool afterwards.

Accepted entries are immediately active — the server must be restarted to reload the corpus if the docent is already running.

Tips

  • Start with fix_ entries — they have already been expert-corrected and are the most likely to be ready to accept.
  • For pam_ entries, compare the answer against official Pampanito sources. Common error areas: dates, pay rates, equipment specifics, and numbered lists (counts of torpedoes, heads, engines, etc.).
  • The stats bar (top right of the toolbar) shows how many entries are currently visible, the total count, and how many have already been accepted.
  • Duplicate entries: pam_033 is a known duplicate of pam_021 and should be deleted from the corpus file directly rather than accepted.
