Lost Boats (Eternal Patrol)

Manage the database of US submarines lost on eternal patrol. Search the list on the left by hull number, name, or designation. Select a boat to open its edit form on the right.

  • Click New to add a submarine not yet in the list.
  • Edit the fields and click Save to update the record.
  • Click Delete to permanently remove a record.
  • You can upload a photo for the boat or its commanding officer.
