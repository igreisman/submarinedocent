Incidents

Manage the historical submarine incidents database. Each record describes a notable incident — accidents, losses, collisions, and other events — and is displayed on the public Incidents view.

  • Search the list on the left to filter incidents by keyword.
  • Select an incident to open its edit form on the right.
  • Click New to create a new incident record.
  • Click Save to update the selected record, or Delete to remove it.
